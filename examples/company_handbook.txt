Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook (sample document)

This is a fictional handbook included so you can try the RAG pipeline
immediately. Copy it into the data/ folder or upload it in the app.

1. Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside
core hours employees may arrange their schedule freely, provided they work
40 hours per week. Meetings should not be scheduled on Friday afternoons.

2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require written approval from a department head and are
reviewed every six months. The company reimburses up to 400 EUR once per
employee for home-office equipment.

3. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year and must be used
by 31 March. Leave requests of more than five consecutive days must be
submitted at least four weeks in advance.

4. Sick leave
Employees should notify their manager before 09:30 on the first day of
illness. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three
consecutive working days.

5. Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30
days of purchase, together with a receipt. Expenses above 500 EUR need
manager pre-approval. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

6. Information security
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock
after five minutes. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and stored in
the company password manager. Suspected security incidents must be reported
to security@northwind.example within one hour of discovery.

7. Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses,
conferences and books. Unused budget does not carry over. Two working days
per quarter may be used as dedicated learning days.
